# repoke site: design direction

Set by Pavel (2026-10-28):

- **Logo:** a "repóker de ases", four of a kind in aces, as a play on the name.
- **Colors:** those of playing cards: card-white paper, black and red suits.
- **Style:** minimalist, in the line of brew.sh. It is a command-line utility, so the commands are the content.

Decisions taken from that direction (agent, approved with the first version or to be revised):

- Palette: paper `#FBF9F4`, ink `#17140F`, card red `#B3121F` as the one accent (links, the hearts and diamonds, the copy button's feedback). Dark theme: the green felt of a card table, `#0F2A22`, with paper-white ink and a lighter red `#FF6B6B` that keeps AA on felt.
- Type: a classic serif (Iowan Old Style / Palatino / Georgia, system fonts) for the wordmark and headings, echoing card indices; the system sans for prose; the system monospace for commands. No web fonts: the page should load as fast as the tool runs.
- Identity motif: the four suits mark the four main sections, in their own colors, the way they mark the four aces in the logo.
- Dials: ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1 (hover and focus states only).
- Evidence over claims: real command output only, copied from a real run, and no numbers that aren't facts about repoke itself.
